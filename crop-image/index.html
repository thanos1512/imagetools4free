How to crop an image

  1. Upload your picture. Its full size in pixels is shown above the settings.
  2. Enter X (how far from the left edge to start) and Y (how far from the top to start).
  3. Enter the width and height of the area you want to keep.
  4. Press Crop and download to save the cropped area as a PNG.

Understanding X, Y, width and height

Picture positions are measured in pixels from the top-left corner. X = 0 and Y = 0 is that corner. If your image is 1000 × 800 pixels and you want the middle 500 × 400 area, start at X = 250 and Y = 200, with width 500 and height 400.

To crop to a square from the centre of a 1200 × 800 photo, use width and height 800, X = 200 and Y = 0.

Crop first, then resize

To get an image with an exact size such as 1080 × 1080 without stretching, crop it to the right shape first (a square in this case), then use the Image Resizer to change it to the final size.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. The cropped area is saved as PNG with every pixel unchanged.

How do I find the size of my image?

It is shown above the crop settings after you upload. You can also use our Image Info tool.

What if my crop area goes past the edge?

Any part outside the picture will be transparent in the download, so keep X + width and Y + height within the image size.

Is the cropper free?

Yes. It is free, needs no signup and adds no watermark.
