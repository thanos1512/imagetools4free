How to rotate or flip an image

  1. Upload your picture. A live preview appears on the right.
  2. Choose a rotation: 90°, 180° or 270° (clockwise).
  3. Press Horizontal to mirror the picture left to right, or Vertical to flip it upside down. You can combine rotation and flipping.
  4. When the preview looks right, press Download image. The image is saved as a PNG.

Why is my photo sideways?

Phones save photos in one direction and add a small note inside the file saying which way is up. Some apps and websites ignore that note, so the photo appears sideways or upside down. Rotating the picture here and saving it again fixes the image itself, so it looks right everywhere.

Rotate or flip: what is the difference?

Rotating turns the whole picture around its centre, like turning a printed photo on a table. Flipping creates a mirror image. Use a horizontal flip to fix selfies where text appears backwards, or to make a person face the other way in a design.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating an image lower its quality?

No. The download is saved as PNG, which keeps every pixel exactly as it was.

Can I rotate by a custom angle like 45°?

Not at the moment. The tool supports 90°, 180° and 270°, which covers fixing sideways and upside-down photos.

Which way does 90° rotate?

90° turns the picture a quarter turn clockwise. Use 270° for a quarter turn counter-clockwise.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, and your photo stays on your device.
