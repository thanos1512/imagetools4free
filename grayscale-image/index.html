How to make an image black and white

  1. Upload a colour picture.
  2. Choose a Method. Luma is recommended for natural-looking results.
  3. Press Convert and download to save the black and white version as a PNG.

The three methods explained

Luma weighs the colours the way human eyes see brightness: green counts most, then red, then blue. This gives the most natural black and white photo and is the best choice for most pictures.

Average adds the red, green and blue values together and divides by three. It is simple, but can make some colours look too dark or too light, which gives a flatter look.

Desaturate takes the middle point between the brightest and darkest colour in each pixel. It often gives a softer, lower-contrast result.

Tips for better black and white photos

Pictures with strong light and shadow, clear shapes and interesting textures usually look best in black and white. Portraits, architecture and street photos are good candidates. If a result looks dull, try another method, or start from a photo with more contrast.

Frequently asked questions

Is grayscale the same as black and white?

In everyday use, yes. A grayscale image uses shades of grey from black to white instead of colours.

Can I turn the grayscale image back into colour?

No. The colour information is removed. Keep your original file if you might want the colour version later.

Does it work with transparent PNGs?

Yes. Transparent areas stay transparent in the downloaded PNG.
