How to edit a photo with filters

  1. Choose a photo. A live preview appears on the right.
  2. Tap a look such as Vivid, Warm or Vintage for a quick result.
  3. Fine-tune with the sliders. Press and hold Hold to compare to see the original.
  4. Choose JPG or PNG and press Download photo. The download keeps your photo's full size.

What each slider does

SliderWhat it changes
BrightnessMakes the whole photo lighter or darker. Helpful for photos taken indoors or at night.
ContrastIncreases or reduces the difference between light and dark areas.
SaturationMakes colours stronger or weaker. All the way left gives black and white.
WarmthAdds a golden, sunny tone or a cool, blue tone.
FadeSoftens the darkest and brightest parts for a gentle, film-like look.
VignetteDarkens the corners to draw attention to the centre.

Fixing common photo problems

Too dark: raise Brightness a little and add some Contrast so it does not look washed out. Dull colours: raise Saturation by 15–30. Blue indoor light: move Warmth to the right. Harsh, too bright: lower Contrast or add a little Fade. Small changes usually look more natural than big ones.

Frequently asked questions

Does the filter reduce my photo's size?

No. The preview is smaller so it stays fast, but the download is made from your full-size photo (up to about 24 megapixels).

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All editing happens in your browser.

Which format should I save as?

JPG gives a smaller file and is best for sharing. PNG keeps every detail and is best if you will edit the photo again.

Can I combine a look with my own changes?

Yes. Pick a look first, then move any slider to adjust it.
