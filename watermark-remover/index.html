How to remove a watermark or object from a photo

  1. Choose your photo. It appears on the right.
  2. Set the brush size, then paint over the date stamp, text, logo or object you want to remove. A pink layer shows where you painted.
  3. Press Erase painted area. The tool fills the area using the colours around it.
  4. If a faint mark is left, paint over it again and erase once more. Use Undo if you change your mind.
  5. Press Download image to save the result.

What it works best on

The remover fills each painted area using the colours just outside it. That makes it very good at removing small things on simple backgrounds: a date stamp in the corner of a photo, a line of text over sky or a wall, a dust spot, a power line, or a small logo. Paint a little beyond the edges of the mark so no outline is left behind.

Large objects on busy, detailed backgrounds, such as a person standing in front of a crowd, are much harder. The fill can look smooth or smudged there. For the best results, remove big objects in a few smaller passes rather than all at once.

Use it on pictures you are allowed to edit

Watermarks protect the work of photographers, artists and stock image sites. Removing someone else's watermark and using their picture without permission can break copyright law and the image's licence. Use this tool on your own photos, for example to remove an old watermark you added yourself or a date stamp from your camera, or on images whose owner has allowed you to edit them. If you need a stock photo without a watermark, buy a licence from the site that sells it.

Tips for a cleaner result

  • Use a brush only slightly bigger than the mark you are removing.
  • Work on one mark at a time instead of painting everything at once.
  • Zoom out and look at the whole photo after each pass. Small leftovers are easier to spot from a distance.
  • Keep a copy of the original in case you want to start again.

Frequently asked questions

Is the watermark remover free?

Yes. There is no signup, no limit and nothing is added to your photo.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The removal happens in your browser on your own device, so your photo is never sent anywhere.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Paint with your finger on the photo. A larger brush is easier to control on a small screen.

Will the download be lower quality?

No. The photo keeps its size unless it is extremely large (over about 16 megapixels), and only the painted areas are changed.
