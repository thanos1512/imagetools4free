How to convert an image

  1. Upload the picture you want to convert. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP are accepted.
  2. Choose the Output Format: JPEG, PNG or WebP.
  3. For JPEG and WebP, set the quality. 90% keeps the image looking almost identical to the original.
  4. Press Convert & Download to save the new file.

Which format should I use?

FormatBest forTransparency
JPEG (.jpg)Photos, sharing, email, forms that only accept JPGNo
PNG (.png)Logos, screenshots, graphics with text, images that need a see-through backgroundYes
WebP (.webp)Images on websites, where small files load fasterYes

Converting WebP to JPG

Many pictures saved from websites are WebP files, and some older apps and upload forms do not accept them. Upload the WebP file, choose JPEG, and download. The converted JPG opens in any program.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPEG cannot store transparency, so when you convert a PNG or WebP with a see-through background to JPEG, the transparent parts are filled with white. Choose PNG or WebP if you want to keep the background transparent. GIF animations are converted from the first frame only.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting reduce image quality?

Converting to PNG keeps full quality. Converting to JPEG or WebP uses the quality you choose; at 90% or above the difference is very hard to see.

Can I convert PNG to JPG for free?

Yes. Upload the PNG, choose JPEG and download. There is no limit and no watermark.

Can I convert HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Only if your browser can open HEIC files, which Safari on Apple devices can. On other browsers, set your iPhone camera to 'Most Compatible' to save photos as JPG.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion is done by your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.
