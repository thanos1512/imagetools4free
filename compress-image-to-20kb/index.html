How to compress an image to 20 KB

  1. Choose your photo. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP all work.
  2. The Target file size is already set to Under 20 KB.
  3. Press Compress image. The tool tries different quality levels and, if needed, makes the picture slightly smaller in pixels until the file fits.
  4. Check the result size and press Download image.

Where a 20 KB limit is used

A 20 KB limit is common for signature images and small ID photos on application forms, exam registrations and some bank and government portals. 20 KB is very small, so the tool usually has to reduce the picture's dimensions as well as its quality. For a signature or a passport-style photo, that is fine, because these images are shown small anyway.

Tips for the best result

  • Crop first. Cut away empty background with the Image Cropper, so the pixels are spent on your face or signature.
  • Signatures: sign with a dark pen on plain white paper and photograph it in good light. Clean, high-contrast signatures stay sharp even at 20 KB.
  • Check the minimum. Some forms also need at least 10 KB. If yours does, type a number like 18 in the Target file size box so the file stays between the limits.
  • If the form asks for exact pixels, such as 200 × 230, resize to that size first with the Image Resizer, then come back here.

Frequently asked questions

Will my photo still look clear at 20 KB?

For a passport-style photo or a signature, yes. These are usually displayed at around 200–400 pixels wide, and 20 KB is enough for that. A large landscape photo will look soft at 20 KB.

Why did the tool make my image smaller in pixels?

Below a certain quality, JPG images start to look blocky. Making the picture a little smaller in pixels keeps it clean while reaching 20 KB.

Can I get exactly 20 KB?

The tool makes the file as close to 20 KB as it can without going over, usually 18–20 KB. Forms accept anything under the limit.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.
