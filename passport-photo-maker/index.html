How to make a passport photo

  1. Choose a clear photo of yourself, facing the camera, against a plain light wall.
  2. Pick your photo size, such as 35 × 45 mm or 2 × 2 inch. The box on your photo changes to that exact shape.
  3. Move the box so your face fits inside the dashed oval. Drag the pink corners to make your face bigger or smaller in the photo.
  4. Press Download photo for a single photo to upload, or Download print sheet to print several copies.

Tips for a photo that gets accepted

  • Background: stand in front of a plain white or light grey wall, about half a metre away, so no shadow falls behind you.
  • Light: face a window or a soft light so both sides of your face are evenly lit.
  • Pose: look straight at the camera with a neutral expression and your mouth closed. Keep your hair away from your face.
  • Glasses and hats: many countries no longer allow glasses in passport photos. Remove hats unless worn for religious reasons.
  • Camera: ask someone else to take the photo from about 1.5 metres away at eye level. Selfies held at arm's length can distort the face.

Uploading to an online form

Many online applications ask for a photo under a certain file size, such as 50 KB or 100 KB. Type that number in Maximum file size before downloading, and the photo will fit while keeping the exact size in pixels. For other limits, the Image Compressor and the guide to photos for online forms can help.

Frequently asked questions

Will my passport photo be accepted?

This tool makes the photo the correct size and shape, but each country also has rules about the background colour, head size, expression and how recent the photo is. Always check the latest official rules for your document before you submit or print.

Can it change the background to white?

Not automatically. For the best result, take the photo in front of a plain white or light grey wall, with even light and no shadows behind you.

How do I print the photos?

Download the print sheet and print it on 4 × 6 inch photo paper at a photo shop or at home, or on A4 paper. Choose 100% or "actual size" in the print settings, not "fit to page", then cut along the light grey lines.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.
