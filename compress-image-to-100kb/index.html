How to compress an image to 100 KB

  1. Choose your photo. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP all work.
  2. The File size goal is already set to Under 100 KB.
  3. Press Compress image. The tool tries different quality levels and, if needed, makes the picture slightly smaller in pixels until the file fits.
  4. Check the result size and press Download image.

Where a 100 KB limit is used

A 100 KB limit is common for photos and scanned documents on application forms, for images in website contact forms and listings, and for keeping email attachments small. At 100 KB, a normal photo can usually stay around 1000–1200 pixels wide and still look sharp on a phone or laptop screen.

Tips for the best result

  • Scanned documents: crop away the table or floor around the paper first. The tool then has more space for the text, which keeps it readable.
  • For websites, choose WebP as the format if your site supports it. It looks better than JPG at the same size.
  • If your photo is already under 100 KB as a JPG, the tool tells you and keeps it as it is.
  • Need a few images under 100 KB in one PDF? Compress them here, then combine them with Image to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How much quality is lost at 100 KB?

For a normal photo viewed on screen, very little. The tool keeps the highest quality that fits and only reduces the pixel size if it has to.

Can I compress a PNG screenshot to 100 KB?

Yes. It is saved as a JPG or WebP, which is much smaller than PNG for most screenshots. Transparent areas become white in JPG.

What if I need under 150 KB or 200 KB?

Choose a different goal in the File size goal menu, or pick Custom size and type any number of KB.

Is my image stored anywhere?

No. It is processed in your browser and never uploaded.
