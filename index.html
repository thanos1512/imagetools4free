Getting a photo ready for a website

Photos straight from a phone are often far bigger than a web page needs. A reliable order is to crop the picture to the shape you want, resize it to the width it will be displayed at, and then compress it. Doing them in that order usually gives the smallest file without a visible loss in quality.
