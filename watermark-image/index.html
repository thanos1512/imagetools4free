How to add a watermark to a photo

  1. Upload your photo. A live preview appears on the right.
  2. Type your watermark text, for example your name, website or brand.
  3. Adjust the Font Size, Opacity, Position and Color. The preview updates as you go.
  4. Press Download with Watermark to save the result as a PNG.

Why add a watermark?

A watermark shows who made a picture and makes it harder for others to reuse it without credit. Photographers, artists, online sellers and social media creators often add their name or website to images before posting them.

Tips for a good watermark

Keep the text short, such as your name or website address. An opacity of 30–50% is usually visible without hiding the photo. White text works well on dark photos and black text on light photos; use the colour picker to match your brand. A corner position keeps the subject clear, while the centre position gives the strongest protection against cropping.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a logo image as a watermark?

Not yet. The tool currently adds text watermarks only.

Can I type the © symbol?

Yes. You can type or paste any symbol, including © and ®, into the watermark text box.

Does the tool add its own watermark?

No. Only the text you type is added. The default text is just an example you can replace.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The watermark is added in your browser on your device.
