How to pick a colour from an image

  1. Choose a picture, such as a logo, a screenshot or a photo.
  2. Move over the picture to preview colours. Click to pick one.
  3. Copy the HEX, RGB or HSL code with the Copy button.
  4. Use the Main colours palette to grab the most common colours in the picture with one click.

HEX, RGB and HSL explained

HEX codes like #2c4bdb are used in websites, CSS, Canva and most design apps. RGB describes the amount of red, green and blue from 0 to 255, and is common in code and office programs. HSL describes hue (the colour on a colour wheel), saturation (how strong it is) and lightness, which makes it easy to create lighter or darker versions of the same colour.

Matching brand colours

If you are designing a poster, a social media post or a website and want it to match an existing logo, upload the logo and pick its main colour. Logos saved as JPG can have slightly different pixels near edges, so click in the middle of a solid area for the truest colour.

Frequently asked questions

Is the colour picker free?

Yes, with no limits and no signup.

Why is the colour slightly different from the original design?

JPG compression and photo lighting change colours a little. Pick from a large, flat area, or use the original PNG or SVG file if you have it.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Colours are read in your browser on your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Tap the picture to pick a colour.
