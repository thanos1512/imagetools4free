Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Short version: We do NOT collect, upload or store any of your images. All processing happens 100% in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

1. Who We Are

ImageTools4Free (imagetools4free.vercel.app) is a free browser-based image tools website offering tools for compressing, resizing, converting, cropping, editing and combining images.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT upload your images to any server
  • We do NOT store your images anywhere
  • We do NOT collect your name, email or personal details
  • We do NOT require any account or signup
  • We do NOT sell data to third parties

3. How Our Tools Work

All image processing happens entirely inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Images are processed locally on your device and downloaded directly to you. No image data is ever sent to our servers.

4. Google AdSense, Advertising & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps the tools free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Fonts

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, which can see your IP address. Google says it does not use this to build advertising profiles. See Google Fonts privacy.

Log data and analytics

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) may automatically record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working. This information is not linked to the images you process, which never leave your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
