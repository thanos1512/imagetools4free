How to convert images to PDF

  1. Choose one or more images. You can select several at once and add more later.
  2. Check the order of the pages in the list. Use the arrows to move a page up or down, or × to remove it.
  3. Pick a page size (A4, US Letter or the size of each image), the orientation and the margin.
  4. Press Create PDF, then Download PDF.

When to use Image to PDF

Many forms and offices ask for documents as a single PDF. This tool is handy for putting scanned pages or phone photos of a document into one file, sending several receipts together, submitting an assignment, or sharing a set of photos that everyone can open on any device.

Keeping the PDF small

Each image is stored in the PDF as a JPG. The Image quality slider controls how much it is compressed. 85% keeps text and photos clear while keeping the file reasonably small. If a website says your PDF is too large, lower the quality to 60–70%, or resize the images before adding them.

Frequently asked questions

How many images can I put in one PDF?

There is no fixed limit. Dozens of photos work fine on a computer; on older phones, very large batches may be slow.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The PDF is built in your browser, on your device.

Do transparent PNG images work?

Yes. Transparent areas become white in the PDF.

Can I convert a PDF back to images?

Not with this tool yet. It only turns images into a PDF.
