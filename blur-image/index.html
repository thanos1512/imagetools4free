How to blur or pixelate part of an image

  1. Choose your photo.
  2. Pick a style: Pixelate turns the area into large squares, and Blur makes it soft and smooth.
  3. Drag across each face, name, address or number plate you want to hide. You can select as many areas as you need.
  4. Adjust Strength until the details can no longer be read.
  5. Press Download image.

Protect privacy before you share

Screenshots and photos often show more than we mean to share: a phone number in a chat, a home address on a parcel, a bank detail on a receipt, a child's school name, or a car number plate. Hiding these before you post online or send a picture to a group helps protect you and other people.

Pixelate or blur?

Pixelate is better for hiding text and numbers, because the squares remove the shapes of letters. Use a high strength so each square is bigger than a letter. Blur looks softer and is often used for faces and backgrounds. Choose Whole image to blur the entire picture, for example to create a soft background for text.

Make sure the hidden details cannot be read

A weak blur can sometimes still be read, especially on short numbers. Always zoom in on the result before sharing. For very sensitive information like passwords or card numbers, the safest option is not to share that part of the picture at all: crop it out instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is blurring done on my device?

Yes. Your photo is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Can someone undo the blur or pixelation?

Not from the downloaded file, because the original pixels are replaced. With weak settings, though, some text may still be guessed, so use a strong setting for text.

Can I select more than one area?

Yes. Drag across each area in turn. Use Undo last area to remove the most recent one.

Does it work on screenshots?

Yes. It works on any picture your browser can open, including PNG screenshots.
